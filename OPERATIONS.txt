=====================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING — when something looks broken
=====================================================================

SYMPTOM: Client says "site is slow / won't open" (first tap)
  → Normal if you haven't done Setup #1 (keep-alive ping).
  → After 1-2 min retry it loads fast. Set up the ping to avoid this.

SYMPTOM: Site won't open at all (even after minutes)
  → 1. Go to https://dashboard.render.com
  → 2. Your service card should say "Live".
       - If it says "Error" or "Failed": tap it → "Logs" tab →
         send me a screenshot of the red errors.
       - If it says "Deploying": it's just deploying — wait 2 min.
  → 3. If the service card is "Live" but the page doesn't load,
     send me a screenshot of what your client sees.

SYMPTOM: A deploy broke the site (after you changed a file)
  → ONE-CLICK FIX: dashboard.render.com → your service → "Deployments"
     tab → find the latest (red/failed or the one that broke it) →
     tap the "↩ Rollback" button. Site reverts to the last good
     version in ~1 minute.

SYMPTOM: Client's page shows "Couldn't read that link / invalid"
  → Their provider link is expired or was retyped wrong.
  → They open their personal page again, paste the FRESH link,
     get a new personal URL. (Or use your Seller Tool to re-generate.)

SYMPTOM: Page loads but says "quota text not recognized"
  → Your provider changed their status format. Send me a fresh
     provider link — I'll update the parser, you upload the new
     apex-server.py to GitHub, done in ~2 min.

SYMPTOM: "No VLESS nodes found"
  → Provider changed node format/protocol. Same fix: send me a
     fresh link, I update the converter.
